The courtyard between three residential blocks in central Dnipro was damaged in early 2023 and left fenced off for over a year. Working with the Dnipro Children's Fund, we cleared debris, resurfaced the ground, and installed play equipment sourced from a Ukrainian manufacturer to keep the project's money circulating locally.

A rota of two trained facilitators now supervises structured play sessions three afternoons a week, alongside informal, unsupervised use by neighbourhood families the rest of the time.

Sessions are deliberately low-key: no counting, no press visits during opening hours, no requirement that families explain why they're there.

Funded by individual monthly donors through the **Psychosocial Support** programme.
