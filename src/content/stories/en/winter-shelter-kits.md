Between November and February, our logistics team worked with municipal facilities staff to insulate 37 shared basement shelters across two oblasts — the ones families use during air-raid alerts, sometimes for hours at a time.

Each shelter received wall insulation panels, battery lighting, basic first-aid supplies and a carbon-monoxide detector. We prioritised sites serving buildings with a high concentration of families with young children, based on lists provided by local housing administrations.

This work sits under the **Winter Shelter & School Kits** campaign, funded primarily by corporate matched-giving partners — details on the [Get Involved](/en/get-involved/) page.
