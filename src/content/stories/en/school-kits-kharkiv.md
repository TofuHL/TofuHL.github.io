Ahead of the new term, our field team and eleven local volunteers packed and delivered 1,400 school kits across four districts of Kharkiv Oblast. Each kit contains a backpack, notebooks, a pencil case and — new this year — a fleece layer, after teachers told us classrooms are often heated only a few hours a day.

Distribution took place at three community centres over five days, coordinated with school administrators so that kits reached children directly rather than sitting in storage.

"We stopped asking whether a school is 'safe enough' and started asking whether it's open," says field coordinator Yuliia Bondar. "If it's open, children need to be equipped for it."

The programme is funded through the **Winter Shelter & School Kits** campaign — see current progress on the [Impact & Fundraising](/en/impact/) page.

No child pictured in field photography from this delivery is identified by name, school, or exact address, in line with our [safeguarding policy](/en/transparency/#safeguarding).
